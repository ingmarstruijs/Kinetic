Kinetic Kids shows chores assigned from Kinetic Link.
Children mark tasks complete and earn XP. There is no account and no
telemetry. Local task data is stored in an encrypted SQLite database.

Enrollment uses a family QR from the link app; the WebDAV password is
typed on the kids device (it is not embedded in the QR). Assigned tasks
sync from your own HTTPS WebDAV server and are encrypted with the family key.

Requires camera permission only for enrollment QR scanning.
WebDAV sync requires HTTPS.
