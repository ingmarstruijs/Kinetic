Kinetic Link helps families manage tasks and notes without accounts or
telemetry. Data stays on your device in an encrypted SQLite database and
can optionally sync to your own HTTPS WebDAV server (for example Nextcloud)
using AES-256-GCM encryption.

Features:
• Personal tasks and markdown notes
• Partner proposals via QR pairing
• Kids chores with XP rewards (use with Kinetic Kids)
• Offline suggestion heuristics (no cloud AI)
• Encrypted .kvault backups

Requires camera permission only for QR pairing/enrollment.
WebDAV sync requires HTTPS.
